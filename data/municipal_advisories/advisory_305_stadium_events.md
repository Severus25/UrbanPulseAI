# Municipal Advisory 305: Special Event Traffic Management (Stadium Zone)

**Effective Date:** March 15, 2025  
**Issuer:** City Operations and Safety Council

## 1. Scope
This advisory applies to the **Stadium District** (within a 1.5 km radius of the Central Arena) during any event with an expected attendance exceeding **20,000 people**.

## 2. Event-Day Routing Rules
* Beginning **2 hours prior** to the event start and lasting until **1.5 hours post-event**, the following road closures are mandated:
  * **Arena Way (Segment 102)**: Closed to all non-emergency, personal vehicles.
  * **Broadway Corridor (Segment 105)**: Right turn onto Arena Way is restricted to transit buses and registered shuttles only.
* Secondary arterials will act as detour routes. Recommended bypasses include **North Ring Road (Segment 88)** and **South Express Link (Segment 92)**.
* Traffic signals along Broadway Corridor must extend green cycles by **15 seconds** for north-south traffic to absorb high attendance outflows.
