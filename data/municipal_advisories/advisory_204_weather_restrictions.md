# Municipal Advisory 204: Inclement Weather Traffic Regulations

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Issuer:** Municipal Department of Transportation (MDOT)

## 1. Speed Reductions
* When rainfall exceeds **10mm/hour** or visibility drops below **100 meters**, speed limits on all major arterial roads (including freeway bypasses) are automatically reduced by **20 km/h**.
* Violators will face double penalties for speeding under hazardous conditions.

## 2. Lane Closures and Vehicle Type Restrictions
* Heavy vehicles (exceeding 5 tons) are restricted to the rightmost lane on all two-lane or three-lane arterial segments during active storm alerts.
* Low-lying underpasses under active flood alerts (greater than 15mm/hour precipitation forecast) must block the leftmost lane to prevent passenger vehicles from entering deep water pooling zones.
