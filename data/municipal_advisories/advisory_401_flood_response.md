# Municipal Advisory 401: Standard Flood Response and Infrastructure Closures

**Effective Date:** May 10, 2025  
**Issuer:** Flood Control and Civil Protection Agency

## 1. Monitoring Thresholds
Low-lying road segments adjacent to the river canal are designated as **High-Risk Flood Zones**.
* **Stage 1 (Precipitation >15mm/hour)**: Underpasses on **River Valley Road (Segment 42)** and **Underpass Drive (Segment 77)** must be placed under continuous visual and sensor monitoring.
* **Stage 2 (Water Level >10cm on road)**: The road segment must be immediately closed to civilian traffic. Detours must route vehicles away from the valley floor.

## 2. Emergency Detour Routing
* During Stage 2 closures of Segment 42, all vehicles must be rerouted through the **Elevated Bypass (Segment 114)**.
* Traffic reasoners and routing agents must recommend the Elevated Bypass and explicitly state that River Valley Road is under active flood closure.
